# ⚖️ Mutabiq (مطابق): AI-Powered RegTech SaaS Platform

## 📌 Project Overview
**Mutabiq** is a B2B AI-powered Regulatory Technology (RegTech) SaaS platform tailored for small and mid-sized FinTech companies in Saudi Arabia—specifically startups operating within the **SAMA Regulatory Sandbox** or newly licensed financial institutions. Because Saudi financial regulations are extensive, frequently updated, and published primarily in Arabic, Mutabiq automates compliance management by comparing internal company policies against SAMA rules and guidelines using an **Arabic-native AI engine**.

## 🚀 Core Products & Platform Features
* **AI Policy Gap Analysis:** Uploads internal policies and product plans to automatically detect missing regulatory requirements and generate prioritized remediation steps.
* **Inspection-Ready Reports:** Produces structured, audit-ready compliance documentation with readiness scoring (e.g., 72/100 controls reviewed) to prepare teams for SAMA inspections.
* **Bilingual AI Regulatory Chatbot:** An Arabic/English conversational assistant grounded in official Saudi regulatory frameworks (SAMA Cyber Security Framework, Open Banking Policy, AML/CTF Rules, and PDPL) with source citations.
* **Regulatory Library & Change Alerts:** A searchable repository of SAMA, NCA, and SDAIA frameworks paired with priority-ranked (High/Medium/Low) automated update notifications.
* **Peer Benchmarking & Legal Add-ons:** Anonymized compliance benchmarking against industry peers, alongside optional human legal review from partnered Saudi law firms.

## 🌐 Bilingual Prototype & Business Architecture
* **Full RTL/LTR Support:** Designed and prototyped a complete bilingual web interface (English & Arabic) featuring a client compliance workspace, interactive regulatory chatbot, searchable library, tiered SaaS pricing (Starter, Growth, Enterprise), and shopping cart flow.
* **Data Residency & Security:** Architected around Saudi-hosted cloud infrastructure to strictly comply with the **Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)**, **SAMA Cyber Security Framework**, and **NCA** controls.
* **Comprehensive Business Plan:** Developed a full go-to-market strategy based in **KAFD (Riyadh)**, covering competitive analysis (benchmarking against Mozn and STAMP), subscription monetization (Moyasar/HyperPay integration), operational SaaS architecture, HR structure, and a 2026–2028 product roadmap.
